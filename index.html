02 / Selected work

Systems designed to move data smarter.

Research and implementation across memory-centric architectures, accelerators, and real-time systems.

01

CXL · Near-memory processing

RAG similarity-search accelerator

Designed synthesizable Verilog RTL for a CXL-based accelerator that computes FP16 dot products and maintains top-K candidates near memory. Verified through simulation, synthesized with Synopsys Design Compiler, and evaluated for area, power, and timing at 1 GHz.

  • Verilog
  • CXL
  • FP16
  • Synopsys DC
02

HBM · Chiplets · LLM inference

Memory systems for AI decode

Contributed to a custom multi-side-PHY HBM architecture addressing bandwidth, shoreline, and capacity limits. Integrated NPU, memory, and NoC simulators to study throughput, latency, bandwidth, package communication, and thermal behavior.

  • HBM
  • NoC
  • ONNXim
  • BookSim
03

FPGA · DRAM · Protocol offload

SmartDIMM acceleration

Collaborated on the RTL design of a Samsung AxDIMM accelerator for upper-layer protocol operations on DRAM-resident data, including encryption, decryption, compression, and decompression.

  • AxDIMM
  • FPGA
  • RTL
  • Ramulator
